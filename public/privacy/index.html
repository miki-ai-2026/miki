Privacy

Last updated: October 2026. This page covers the miki.si website only.

What we collect

If you use the early-access form, your email app opens with the details you entered: name, work email, company and number of stores. We receive them only when you send that email. The website does not store form entries itself.

How we use it

We use these details to reply to you about MiKi early access. We do not sell them.

Third parties

  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when the page loads.
  • Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and requested pages.

The MiKi product

MiKi is in development and no store data is processed today. A separate policy covering camera footage and staff data will be published before any store is connected.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email hello@miki.si. Applicable data protection law, for example Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, should be confirmed by a lawyer before launch.
